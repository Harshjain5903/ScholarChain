ScholarChain gold-set labeler

Do not label from the DBLP numeric suffix (e.g. "0004") or from ORCID equality alone. Those are the matcher's own signals, so labeling from them leaks the matcher into the ground truth. Check the papers, profiles and affiliations; use UNSURE when only those signals remain. See modules/entity-resolution/GOLD-SET-LABELING.md. This page makes no network requests; links open in a new tab only when you click them.
Keys: 1 = MATCH, 2 = NON_MATCH, 3 = UNSURE, ← previous, → next (shortcuts are off while typing in notes). Clicking the active label again clears it.
